Task Cycle

Personal project

Recurring chores that follow real life. The clock restarts when you actually finish a task.

React · TypeScript · Capacitor · PWA

A closer look

Finish a three-day chore on Tuesday and it’s due on Friday. Finish it late on Sunday and the next date moves to Wednesday. Editing a completion in your history updates the schedule too.

Tasks stay on the device. The app includes native reminders, import and export, and Portuguese and English interfaces.

Safe spending guard

Testnet research

Small everyday transfers take a simpler path. Larger transfers require extra approval or a cancellable delay.

React · TypeScript · Solidity · Safe · WebAuthn

A closer look

The interface explains which spending tier a transfer falls into, the approval it needs and when it can execute. The policy is enforced through Safe guards and a delay module.

This is an unaudited research prototype on a test network. The current browser implementation uses a local backend for provisioning and submissions.
